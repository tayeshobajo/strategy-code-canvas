# Trust Tai — Project Design Doctrine

The principles governing how every client intake becomes a real, coherent, lasting outcome.

Created: 2026-07-11.

---

## What This Is

This is not a process checklist. It is a worldview. Every agent that touches a Trust Tai project operates under these principles. The doctrine does not change per project. The application of it does.

---

## Rule 1 — Truth Before Direction

Point A is not where the client thinks they are. It is where they actually are — including what they have not said, the assumptions they are carrying, and the constraints they have not named.

We do not generate Point B until Point A is honest.

## Rule 2 — The Problem Behind the Problem

Clients describe symptoms. Strategists diagnose causes. The intake is not a brief. It is a diagnostic conversation. We always diagnose before we prescribe.

## Rule 3 — Point B Must Be Specific Enough to Be Wrong

If Point B cannot be wrong, it is not Point B. It is a direction. Point B must have a number, a timeframe, and a mechanism. All three.

## Rule 4 — Constraints Are Design Inputs, Not Obstacles

Every constraint gets named explicitly in the Spine before roadmap generation begins. If it is not named, it will surface as a surprise later.

## Rule 5 — Sequence Is Strategy

Every roadmap must show not just what gets built, but why in this order. Each phase unlocks the next. That dependency chain is the strategy.

## Rule 6 — The Roadmap Serves the Founder, Not the Agency

Every roadmap gets a one-paragraph plain language summary at the top. If we cannot write it, the roadmap is not ready.

## Rule 7 — Evidence Is Not Optional

Every claim in the Understanding brief must be classified: Known, Inferred, Missing, or Contradictory. Nothing moves to Point B until the classification is done.

## Rule 8 — Captain Does Not Approve Its Own Work

The AI produces. The human decides. Always. Every approval gate has a named human. If there is no named human, the gate cannot close.

## Rule 9 — Drift Is Normal. Hidden Drift Is a Failure.

Every change to an approved decision gets a reason, an approver, and an audit entry. Nothing changes silently.

## Rule 10 — The End Is Not Delivery. It Is Outcome.

Every engagement has a scheduled outcome review. The project is not complete until the outcome is measured against the original Point B.

## Rule 11 — The Platform Inherits the Doctrine

Every agent, every build, every generated artifact operates under these rules. When doctrine and convenience conflict, doctrine wins.
